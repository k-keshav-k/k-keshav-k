## About

I am a **Risk Technology Intern at Moore Capital Management**, where I build Python-based risk tools, data pipelines, APIs, and internal full-stack applications.

Previously, I spent two years as a **Data Scientist at JioSaavn**, developing recommendation, NLP, embedding, moderation, and LLM systems for a large-scale music-streaming platform.

I am also an **MSCS student at NYU Courant**, researching efficient language-model inference and ML-driven systems optimization.

## Selected Work

- **[Phase-Assisted Generation](https://github.com/k-keshav-k/Phase-Assisted-Generation)** — Adaptive decoding for diffusion LMs, reducing forward evaluations by approximately **21%** while maintaining reasoning accuracy.
- **[FlashAttention-2 in Triton](https://github.com/k-keshav-k/Flash-Attention)** — Forward and backward GPU kernels implemented from scratch and profiled with NVIDIA Nsight.
- **[ML-Based Hardware Optimization](https://github.com/k-keshav-k/ML-based-hardware-optimization-using-Phases)** — Workload-phase prediction for dynamic cache and memory-resource management.
- **[Transformer LM from Scratch](https://github.com/k-keshav-k/Large-Language-Models-from-Scratch)** — Complete PyTorch implementation with BPE, RoPE, RMSNorm, SwiGLU, and causal attention.
- **[Alignment and Reasoning RL](https://github.com/k-keshav-k/Alignment-and-Reasoning-RL)** — LLM post-training for mathematical reasoning using supervised fine-tuning and GRPO.
